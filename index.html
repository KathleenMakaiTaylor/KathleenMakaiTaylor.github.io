About Me

I'm a writer with experience across technical and creative content, built on roles spanning U.S., British, and UAE-based organizations in multicultural, multilingual environments. On the technical side, I write SOPs, process documentation, quick start guides, and knowledge base content that hold up for cross-functional and global teams. On the copywriting side, I've written and edited newsletters, social media content, and internal communications, with an eye for tone as much as clarity.
